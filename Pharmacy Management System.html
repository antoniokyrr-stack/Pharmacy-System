Help & Instructions

Returns must be processed within 7 days of the purchase date per DTI regulations.

  1. Search for the original receipt. Enter the receipt number from the top of the customer's receipt.
  2. Review transaction details. Check the purchase date and days left.
  3. Select items to return. Set quantity, item condition and reason.
  4. Proceed. The refund is credited back to the transaction record.
